Things I HATE!!!

  I hate cars, they're inefficent forms of transportation compared to public transit, at least, optimized public transit over optimized car transit. Not only are they inefficent they also pollute the environment, destroy ecosytems with how much space they take up, and are deadly. We did not evolve to drive cars, our reaction times are not good enough for that, which causes a lot of crashes and deaths. But, know what I hate more? Trucks, trucks and SUVs fall under the category of "light truck" in the US, which means they don't have to follow the same safety regulations as regular cars. There boxy frames are easy to produce, but stiff, which can cause injury to the driver if caught in an accident, they also aren't crash compatable with regular cars. This makes them deadly to other drivers if they get in an accident with a truck. Also being so high up makes it harder to see pedestrians closer to you, such as children, when I stand directly in front of a truck, my head barely peaks above hood, and I'm nearly 5'6". But also, trucks nowadays are way too high up, making it harder load and offload materials in the bed. Truck beds have also been getting smaller as these vehicles have transitioned from work vehicles to status symbols for your average American family, as now they have back seats, taking up the precious truck bed space. This transition of trucks becoming a status symbol also makes them bigger, as the bigger the truck, the better you are, which then makes them more dangerous.
